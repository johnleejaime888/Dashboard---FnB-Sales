# Dataset Overview
Dataset contains valuable information that could help us optimize the operations, boost sales, and enhance customer satisfaction. Here's a quick rundown of what you can expect from the dataset:

**Date and Time:** We have meticulously recorded the date and time of each order, allowing us to analyze customer behavior and identify potential peak hours.

**Customer Traffic Analysis:** Analyzing the number of customer visits per day and identifying peak hours with higher customer traffic. This analysis helps uncover customer traffic patterns and supports better staffing and operational planning.

# Analysis

**Bestselling Products:** Analyzing sales data to identify the most popular products among customers. Identifying bestsellers can help inform marketing strategies and prioritize products that have strong customer demand.

**Revenue and Seasonality:** Calculating the total revenue generated throughout the year to provide an overall view of financial performance. Additionally, analyzing sales trends to identify seasonal patterns and determine peak and slow periods, supporting better planning and resource allocation.

**Menu Optimization and Promotions:** Using the data to identify products that are underperforming or receiving little attention.
